DESCRIPTION AND INTERPRETATION
Dark red fill with a darker outline shows final designated critical habitat
for the Everglade snail kite. The saved map filters the USFWS layer to
comname = 'Everglade snail kite'. Beige land, pale blue water, and city labels
provide context. Highlighted areas appear along part of Lake Okeechobee,
inland from southeastern coastal cities, and in smaller patches farther north.
Unshaded areas do not prove species absence or unsuitable habitat. This is
not the complete species range, an estimate of abundance, or a nest map.
